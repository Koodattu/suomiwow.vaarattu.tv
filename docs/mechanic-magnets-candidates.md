# Mechanic magnets — candidate catalogue

Research date: 2026-10-02.

A deliberately oversized catalogue: **148 boss encounters across 26 raids/lairs**, from Midnight through WoD. Sporefall, Crucible of Storms and MoP remain excluded.

Boss names link to encounter sources. **†** means we must isolate the specified avoidable component. These are research candidates; new entries still need their damage spell IDs checked against Mythic WCL events.
