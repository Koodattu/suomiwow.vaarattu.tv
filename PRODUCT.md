# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

SuomiWoW serves Finnish and Nordic World of Warcraft raiders, guild members, community followers, and performance-minded players. They use it to follow current progression, examine character and guild performance, revisit historical raid tiers, and take part in community features without losing the underlying data context.

## Product Purpose

SuomiWoW turns fragmented raid, combat-log, achievement, Mythic+, and community data into a shared history of the Finnish WoW scene. Success means users can understand what happened, recognize the people and guilds involved, and return for both useful analysis and genuinely enjoyable community features. The service and its collectible-card feature remain free, non-tradable, and non-commercial.

## Positioning

The community's raid history, competitive performance, and personalities live together in one transparent, evolving Finnish WoW record.

## Brand Personality

Competitive, playful, and premium. The product voice is direct and knowledgeable about WoW without becoming self-important; moments of spectacle are earned by meaningful results and recognizable community characters.

## Anti-references

SuomiWoW must not feel like monetized gacha, casino software, or a manipulative retention system. Rewards can be exciting, but the interface never implies real-world value, purchasing pressure, or pay-to-progress mechanics.

## Design Principles

- Keep source data and scoring understandable even when the presentation is playful.
- Make community recognition the emotional center: characters, guilds, tiers, and achievements are the story.
- Reserve spectacle for meaningful moments such as a rare pull, a progression kill, or a major rank.
- Preserve raid-tier history instead of silently rewriting past performance with current data.
- Extend the existing SuomiWoW product vocabulary so new features feel native to the same service.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Core workflows must work with keyboard, touch, pointer, screen readers, zoom, and reduced-motion preferences. Text and controls target WCAG AA contrast; color is never the only carrier of grade, finish, score, success, or error state. Finnish and English are maintained together, with layouts allowing for Finnish text expansion.
